Our Activities & Ministries

Committed to serving our community and advancing the Kingdom of God through practical care and spiritual nourishment.

Communion Service: Organised on the first Sunday of every month.

Women's Prayer: Every Wednesday, Mrs. Anand Kumar conducts special prayers for the church, those in need, and those facing persecution.

Kid's Ministry

Reaching out to poor and uneducated children, offering a helping hand while preaching the word of God.

Medical Ministry

Providing medical facilities in remote areas. Through this compassion, the Gospel is introduced, and people experience the love of God.

Educational Ministry

"The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge." We provide educational needs like books and pens to foster dedication among youth.

Pastoral Support

Honoring our founder's vision to encourage struggling pastors, we currently support nearly 30 pastors with Rs. 400 every month.

Youth Empowerment

Teaching biblical values and building self-confidence among young people to help them overcome modern-day addictions and struggles.

Outreach & Special Ministries

Our footprint extends into hospitals, jails, and tribal areas, planting churches and sharing hope where it is needed most.
